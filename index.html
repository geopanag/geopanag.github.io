George Panagopoulos

I am a Research Assistant in Computational Physiology Lab of University of Houston, while previously, I was working for 2 years in the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications of NCSR Demokritos.

My interestes lie in the area of data mining and machine learning. I am particularly interested in interpretable machine learning methods for multivariate time series analysis and a strong advocate of multi-task learning approaches. In terms of applications, I have analyzed coauthorship networks and bibliographic data to extract young authors with high potential, natural language from children essays for creativity modeling in serious games, polysymnography data to decode anxious children sleep problems and currently work with bio-signals from wearable devices monitoring humans driving under stresful conditions. Finally, I am a big fan of Brain Computer Interfaces.
