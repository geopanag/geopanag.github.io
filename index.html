George Panagopoulos

I am a Research Assistant in Computational Physiology Lab of University of Houston, while previously, I was working for 2 years in the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications of NCSR Demokritos.

My interestes lie in the area of data mining and machine learning. I am particularly interested in interpretable machine learning methods for multivariate time series analysis and an enthusiastic fan of multi-task learning algorithms.

In terms of applications and modalities, I have worked with a variety of data, ranging from collaboration networks (graphs) and essays (natural language) to wearable sensors bio-signals(time series).

Finally, I am a Brain Computer Interface enthusiast and am eager to run tutorials or experiments and get my hands dirty with BCI data, whenever I have the chance.

I tend to use R for most of my analysis, but I also employ Python and MATLAB depending on the case. For programming tasks, I use mostly Java and occasionally C++.
