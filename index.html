About Me

Education and Work Experience

I am a machine learning research scientist. I've spent one year working on operations research problems with Nikolaos Liakopoulos in the Algorithms and Optimization lab of Amazon Transportation Services. I received my PhD in graph neural networks and graph algorithms from the Data Science and Mining Team of the Computer Science Laboratory of École Polytechnique, supervised by Prof. Michalis Vazirgiannis and Assistant Prof. Fragkiskos Malliaros . Previously, I was a teaching and research assistant in University of Houston, where I obtained my M.Sc. in computer science advised by Prof. Ioannis Pavlidis. Before that, I was working as a research associate for 2 years in the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications of NCSR Demokritos. I hold a B.Sc. in Informatics and Telematics from Harokopio University of Athens, where I completed my diploma thesis with Assistant Prof. Iraklis Varlamis .

Scientific Interests

My interests lie in relational machine learning, causal inference, and information retrieval. More specifically I am researching machine learning methods for heterogeneous data that can be used for personalization, recommendation, marketing, etc. I am also exploring active learning as well as online learning methods such as multi-armed bandits in social networks and marketplaces. In the recent past, I have worked on combinatorial optimization, forecasting, classification, and epidemic-spreading problems. In terms of applications, I have worked with a variety of data like social, logistics, epidemic, molecular, product, coauthorship, word, and brain graphs. Finally, I am also experienced with NLP in long/short texts and digital signal processing for statistical inference from wearable sensors or neural signals.
