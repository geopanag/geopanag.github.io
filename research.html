  • Analyze bio-signals from wearable devices monitoring humans that drive under stressful conditions, to decode vulnerabilities in human driving.
  • Analyze EEG to decode anxious children with sleep problems, with signal processing techniques and visualizations.
  • Model the creativity of children playing serious games in school, with natural language processing and semantic analysis.
